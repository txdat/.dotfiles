# /ielts-speaking — Prepare and Review

Read [README.md](README.md) and [rubric.md](rubric.md) for assessment or target-band guidance.

## Preparation

Build adaptable ideas and natural spoken language from the learner's topics and experiences. Keep Part 1 answers focused, support Part 2 development, and use explanations/examples for Part 3. Scripts and word counts do not establish actual speaking duration or fluency.

For topic grouping, cluster genuinely reusable material and show how each supplied topic fits, including exceptions. No fixed group count or guaranteed exam coverage applies. For one topic, offer an outline, illustrative response or focused revision, useful contextual expressions, and relevant follow-up practice at the requested depth. Do not present invented personal experiences as the learner's facts.

## Transcript review

Establish the question/part and whether the transcript is verbatim, edited, or automatic. Apply the rubric's transcript-only evidence limits. Locate language problems, explain their effects, and suggest natural spoken alternatives. Do not score an improved script as an independent performance.

Report supported estimates when appropriate, visible coherence, useful corrections, one next exercise, and evidence limits. Focused answer improvement does not require a full assessment. Saving follows README.
