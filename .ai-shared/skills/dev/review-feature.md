# /review-feature — Review a Plan

Read [plan.md](plan.md), [design-feature.md](design-feature.md), and [independence.md](independence.md). Design owns the ambiguity gate, AC/TC/fixture schema, and slicing rules; this review verifies them independently. Entry is `planning`. Unresolved ambiguity is a blocking finding, including in an existing plan that claims to be ready.

## Review in order

1. **Derive independently.** Read the Goal, request, and source contracts before the proposed decisions, ACs, and TCs. Identify required outcomes, prohibited outcomes, failure conditions, and ambiguous terms from that evidence.
2. **Verify resolution and ACs.** Check the plan against the ambiguity gate: why each factor exists, what it resolves, how it is obtained or derived, and the evidence or authority for its resolution. A missing Open Questions section is not proof of resolution. Confirm that resolved behavioral semantics appear in the ACs and every required outcome is represented with decidable Success/Failure. Could all ACs pass while the Goal fails, or reject valid behavior? Report missing, unsupported, or conflicting obligations, including mechanism constraints without a requirement source. For unresolved interpretations, show a concrete scenario and differing consequences; route the finding through the ambiguity gate rather than inventing a user decision.
3. **Verify fixtures and coverage.** Trace every distinct AC obligation to a TC, including side effects, boundaries, and relevant interactions. Check `Proves:` against the scenario's actual purpose and verify each fixture reference and override resolves to sufficient inputs and starting state. Independently derive the expected result from the contract, including any permitted variability. Missing fixture data, an unspecified action, or an undecidable expected result blocks readiness. Apply [design-feature.md — Derive and challenge the spec](design-feature.md#derive-and-challenge-the-spec): demonstrate that the relevant fixtures produce different outcomes for plausible incorrect behavior. Check interactions between identities, cardinalities, values, and modes; one distinguishing fixture need not cover every alternative.
4. **Inspect delivery.** Check TC-to-step mapping, [dependent impacts](../../CODING.md#impact), mechanism invariants, and non-functional commitments against the scope and project conventions. Verify that slices include their tests and implementation and pass and merge in their recorded order without later work.
5. **Reconcile prior findings.** After forming your own judgment, read recorded challenges and review history. Verify claimed corrections and whether recorded safeguards actually defeat the incorrect behavior; a prior verdict is not evidence that a gap is closed.

Record material counterexamples with their target, concrete fixture, differing results, and consequence, or the evidence that defeats them. No fixed attack count or repeated checklist is required. Review TC intent, fixture sufficiency, and expected-result correctness here; [review-code.md](review-code.md) verifies executable arrangements, production entry points, and assertions.

Report `READY` only when the ambiguity gate is satisfied, the Goal is covered, every TC has a sufficient concrete fixture and correct expected result, and required scenarios and delivery obligations are accounted for; otherwise `NEEDS CHANGES`. Give located findings, consequences, and required corrections. Optional preferences do not block. During delivery, the main agent records the verdict/evidence in `## Review History`; READY hands off to [approval.md](approval.md), never directly to implementation.
